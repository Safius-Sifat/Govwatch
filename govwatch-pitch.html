Section 03 · How It Works
01

Crawl

▢ Scrapy + Playwright

Pull e-GP tenders, BG Press gazettes, and ministry PDFs into Cloudflare R2.

  • Scrapy for static PDFs — 1,000 req/min
  • Playwright for ASP.NET state on e-GP
  • Cron every 6 hours via Cloudflare Queues
02

Understand

▢ PaddleOCR · bge-m3

OCR scanned Bangla, split into clause-aware chunks, embed with bge-m3.

  • Bangla OCR on scanned PDFs
  • 1,024-dim multilingual vectors
  • Hybrid: Vectorize + D1 FTS5
03

Expose

▢ Cross-encoder · Neo4j

Re-rank, generate cited answers, and surface anomalies + collusion graphs.

  • bge-reranker-v2-m3 + RRF fusion
  • Price z-score > 2.5 → red flag
  • Shared NID → Neo4j vendor graph
▢ Edge-native · Cloudflare Workers AI · LangGraph orchestration
